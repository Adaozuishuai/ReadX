ReadX v0.1.9 adds citation tools, richer library search, and a faster knowledge graph.

### What's new

- Enrich imported papers with journal, author, and publication year metadata from online sources.
- Open in-text citation links and navigate to the matching reference; export citations and explore citation networks.
- Search the library, compare papers side by side, and view reading statistics.
- Improve the 3D knowledge graph with faster layout, fullscreen controls, paper details, and a readable fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
- Improve translation recovery, PDF rendering, reading navigation, and import progress across Windows and macOS.

### Downloads

- Windows: `ReadX-Setup-0.1.9.exe`
- macOS: download the DMG matching your Mac's architecture. macOS packages are unsigned and may require approval in Privacy & Security on first launch.
