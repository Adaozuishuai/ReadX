ReadX v0.1.7 adds domestic WorkBuddy support and makes paper analysis suitable for research across fields.

### What's new

- Sign in with a domestic Tencent CodeBuddy account to load the account's available WorkBuddy models; no API key is required.
- Generalize paper reading, methods, experiment, results, and reproduction prompts so they adapt to the paper's discipline instead of assuming CBCT or another fixed field.
- Update analysis and reproduction labels to use research design, implementation, analysis methods, and resource costs.

### Downloads

- Windows: `ReadX-Setup-0.1.7.exe`
