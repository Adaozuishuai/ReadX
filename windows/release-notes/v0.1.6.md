ReadX v0.1.6 adds a dedicated presentation workspace and improves paper analysis and reading.

### What's new

- Create academic slide decks from papers in your library, with live slide previews and reusable uploaded PowerPoint templates. The bundled style remains the default theme and is hidden from the template library.
- Read the full paper before planning a deck. Choose from six academic layouts, preserve the aspect ratio of paper figures, paginate wide tables and long content, and keep source references in speaker notes.
- Generate the five deep-reading sections independently and in parallel while reusing the paper context prepared during import. Relevant figures and tables are included in their matching analysis sections.
- Locate recognized figures and tables on the source page for visual verification, and improve PDF annotation boundaries and rendering.
- Improve continuous PDF reading, source/translation navigation, import-state recovery, and background paper-memory preparation.
- Keep Windows and macOS features and interfaces in sync.

### Downloads

- Windows: `ReadX-Setup-0.1.6.exe`
- macOS: download the DMG matching your Mac's architecture. macOS packages are unsigned and may require approval in Privacy & Security on first launch.
